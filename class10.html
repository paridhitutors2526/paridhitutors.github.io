How to Prepare for Class 10

1. Complete NCERT first: Read every chapter carefully and solve all NCERT exercises.

2. Practice regularly: Solve chapter-wise questions after completing each chapter.

3. Solve Previous Year Papers: Practice CBSE papers to understand the question pattern and difficulty level.

4. Attempt Sample Papers: Practice full-length papers within the prescribed examination time.

5. Take Mock Tests: Check your preparation and identify topics that need improvement.
