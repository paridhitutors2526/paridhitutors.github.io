How to Prepare for Class 11

1. Understand the syllabus: Start by understanding your subjects and the complete syllabus.

2. Study NCERT: Read the NCERT textbook carefully and solve the exercises.

3. Make notes: Prepare short notes while studying important concepts.

4. Practice regularly: Solve questions after completing each chapter.

5. Test yourself: Take regular mock tests to identify weak areas.
