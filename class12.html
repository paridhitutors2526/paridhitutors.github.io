🎯 How to Prepare for Class 12 Board Exams

1. Follow the NCERT textbook: Read every chapter carefully and complete all NCERT exercises.

2. Understand the syllabus: Know the complete CBSE syllabus and examination pattern.

3. Practice Previous Year Papers: Solve previous year question papers to understand the type of questions asked.

4. Practice Sample Papers: Attempt complete papers within the examination time limit.

5. Review the Marking Scheme: Learn how answers are evaluated and where marks are awarded.

6. Take Mock Tests: Regular tests will help improve speed, accuracy and confidence.
